# NEVROA brand guidelines (concept)

## Core brand
**NEVROA / GLOBAL INDUSTRIAL SUPPLY**

Brand direction: modern international industrial B2B, confident, precise, understated. This design is a concept pending approval and is not a trademark clearance.

## Colors
| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | `#081B2C` | Headers, hero, footer |
| Steel Blue | `#153A5F` | Supporting UI |
| Metallic Copper | `#C8894B` | Emphasis, links, CTA |
| Cool White | `#F5F6F8` | Section background |
| Ink | `#111827` | Body text |

## Identity assets
- `assets/logo.svg`: white + copper lockup intended for dark surfaces.
- `assets/mark.svg`: standalone N monogram.
- `assets/favicon.svg`: navy-backed square website icon.

## Photography
Use licensed industrial steel, ferroalloy, mineral processing, coal, machinery, and shipping imagery. Do not suggest stock photos show the company's owned facilities or inventory.

## Tone of voice
Accurate, competent, direct and international. Avoid unverified claims about volume, certifications, partners, offices, current stock or established track record.

## Typography
Use a modern sans-serif family with Turkish character support. Current deployable version uses a system sans-serif stack to avoid loading proprietary font files.

## Status
Corporate/contact details and imagery require client sign-off. RFQ is currently email-draft only.
